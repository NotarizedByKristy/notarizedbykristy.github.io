01Our Commitment

We aim for digital accessibility for all visitors and work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We are continually improving the experience on this website.

02Measures We Take

  • Ongoing improvements to site features and content
  • Regular testing and updates
  • Reviewing and acting on feedback from visitors

03Accessibility Features

  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Keyboard navigation
  • Readable contrast between text and background
  • Text that can be resized with your browser
  • Labeled form fields for screen readers

04Known Limitations

Some downloadable documents, such as PDFs, may not yet be fully accessible. Some third-party tools we rely on, such as payment processors and Remote Online Notarization platforms, may not meet the same standard. If you run into one of these, contact us and we will provide an accessible alternative.

05How We Assess

This website is built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We check accessibility with automated tools and manual review.

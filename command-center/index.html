The detail generic apps miss

Your notary fees aren't taxed like the rest of your income.

Fees for notarial acts are excluded from self-employment tax under IRC §1402(c)(1). Travel, printing and the rest of a signing fee are not. The Command Center splits every job for you, using the fee limits your state sets.

Example · one refinance in Texas
Signing fee collected$150.00
Notarial acts in the package15
Texas fee per act× $10
Excluded from SE tax$150.00
SE tax rate (15.3% × 92.35%)14.13%
SE tax you don't owe on this signing$21.19

Across 100 signings like this one, that's about $2,100 a year. The exclusion never exceeds the fee you actually collected. Figures are estimates for bookkeeping; confirm with your tax professional.

Questions

Before you start

Can I cancel QuickBooks?
If you use QuickBooks to invoice, track mileage and estimate taxes, the Command Center does that for your notary work. If your accountant needs QuickBooks for bank reconciliation or payroll, keep it and cancel the notary-specific apps instead. Ask your accountant which files they want; the year-end export is four spreadsheet files.
Is this my notary journal?
No. It's a business tracker. Keep the official journal your state requires, on paper or in an approved electronic journal. The Command Center can record how many acts each job included so your tax math is right.
Where is my data stored?
Only on the devices you use, inside the app. Nothing is sent to a server, and nobody else can see it, including me. That also means you're responsible for backups: the app reminds you, and a full backup takes one tap.
Does it work on my phone and my computer?
Yes. It installs to the Home Screen on iPhone and Android and opens in any current browser on Mac or Windows. Each device keeps its own records, and you move them with a backup file when you want them to match.
My state isn't Texas. Does the tax math still work?
Yes. The self-employment exclusion is federal. Your copy uses your own state's maximum fee per act, which you give me in the intake. If you're not sure of it, check the box and I'll look it up.
Can I build it myself with AI?
Yes. The Command Center Build Kit has the six prompts I used to create it, so you can build and maintain your own version with a paid AI plan. It doesn't include setup or support.
What if my prices change?
Edit them in the Pricing tab whenever you like. Invoices you've already sent keep their original amounts.

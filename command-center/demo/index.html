Fees for performing notarial acts are excluded from self-employment tax under IRC §1402(c)(1) as functions of a public office; they are still subject to ordinary income tax. Travel, printing, coordination and the non-notarial portion of loan-signing fees remain fully SE-taxable. Deposition (court reporting) pay is included in these totals as its own line — it is fully SE-taxable.

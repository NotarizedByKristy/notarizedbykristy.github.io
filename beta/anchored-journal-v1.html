Backup & Restore

Texas requires you to keep these records for 10 years after the notarization (§406.014(g)) — keep your backups that long. A backup file preserves everything exactly, including settings. Restore it on a new phone or after clearing your browser.

Device Storage

Signatures are the only large item stored, and each one is compressed before it is saved. If this ever passes 80%, download a backup before recording more.

Record Integrity

Every entry is sealed with a fingerprint that includes the entry before it. If any earlier entry were altered or removed, this check would fail — proof your record book is intact, verified on your own device with no server involved.
